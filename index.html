Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Connect India CPaaS

Everything you need to know about our Bulk SMS gateway, WhatsApp Business Cloud API, and interactive RCS messaging solutions.

What makes Connect India the leading CPaaS platform in India?

Connect India delivers direct telco carrier routes with sub-2-second OTP delivery, 99.9% uptime SLA, and automated failover routing between SMS, WhatsApp, and RCS to guarantee message delivery.

Is Connect India compliant with TRAI DLT regulations?

Yes, Connect India is a fully certified telemarketer on all major Indian DLT portals (Jio, Airtel, Vodafone Idea, BSNL). We provide complete onboarding assistance for Entity IDs, Headers, and Content Templates.

How does WhatsApp Business API integration work?

As an official Meta Business Partner, we provide instant access to the WhatsApp Cloud API, verified Green Tick verification assistance, interactive message templates, and automated bot workflows.

Why choose RCS messaging over traditional Bulk SMS?

RCS (Rich Communication Services) delivers rich media, clickable call-to-action buttons, verified branding badges, and interactive carousels directly inside Android default message apps, boosting engagement by up to 300%.
