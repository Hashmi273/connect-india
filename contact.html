Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can we integrate Connect INDIA APIs with our existing systems? +
Our REST APIs and SDKs (Node.js, Python, PHP, Java, cURL) are designed for plug-and-play integration. Most engineering teams complete sandbox testing and go live in under 24 to 48 hours with full DLT template synchronization.
Do you provide assistance with DLT Registration and Principal Entity ID? +
Yes! Our dedicated compliance team provides complete end-to-end guidance for DLT registration on Airtel, Jio, and Vodafone-Idea portals, including Header approval and Content Template whitelisting.

Unlike aggregators that route through 3rd-party middlemen, Connect INDIA maintains direct SS7/SMPP and telecom interconnects with major Indian operators, ensuring sub-2-second OTP delivery and true real-time DLR callbacks.

Absolutely. For enterprises broadcasting over 1,000,000 messages per month, we provide tier-based discounted rates, dedicated account management, and custom webhook callbacks.
